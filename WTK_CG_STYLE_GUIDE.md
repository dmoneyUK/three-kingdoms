# WTK CG Style Guide

## 1. Purpose

This guide defines the unified character CG art direction for WTK (War of Three Kingdoms).

The goal is to ensure that all hero illustrations share a consistent visual identity:
- dark
- atmospheric
- elegant
- powerful
- mysterious
- Chinese-fantasy inspired
- suitable for hero cards, splash art, and promotional visuals

This is **not** standard historical realism. It is a stylized, cinematic, high-impact visual system for WTK heroes.

---

## 2. Core Style Definition

WTK hero CG style is defined as:

**Shadow Chinese Fantasy**  
**+ Ink Energy Flow**  
**+ Extreme Chiaroscuro Silhouette**  
**+ Epic Three Kingdoms Hero Art**

### Core style keywords
- dark fantasy
- Chinese fantasy
- epic hero illustration
- cinematic
- atmospheric
- mysterious
- elegant
- majestic
- shadow-driven
- ink-like motion
- high contrast
- silhouette-oriented
- flowing cloth
- symbolic background
- emotionally powerful

---

## 3. Visual Identity Principles

### 3.1 Character-first composition

The hero must always be the main focus.

- Use a **single main character**
- Character should occupy roughly **55%–70%** of the frame
- Prefer **vertical composition**
- Prefer **low-angle heroic view** or **strong cinematic framing**
- The image should feel like **hero splash art / card illustration**

### 3.2 Shadow-first character design

WTK heroes are not designed as plain realistic portraits.

Instead:
- use **half-silhouette**
- use **shadow-covered face**
- use **partial contour lighting**
- use **limited visible facial detail**
- focus on **presence, identity, gesture, weapon, clothing, and energy**

The audience should feel the hero's aura before they study the face.

### 3.3 Mood over realism

Do not prioritize:
- realistic portrait accuracy
- busy historical detail
- documentary realism

Prioritize:
- atmosphere
- symbolic identity
- emotional impression
- visual storytelling
- heroic presence

### 3.4 Abstract yet readable

The design should feel artistic and atmospheric, but still clearly communicate:
- who the character is
- what type of hero they are
- what their temperament is
- what their symbolic role is

---

## 4. Lighting Rules

Lighting is one of the most important elements in WTK style.

### 4.1 Overall lighting language

Use:
- strong contrast
- dramatic backlight
- rim lighting
- moonlight / firelight / energy light
- volumetric haze
- smoke, mist, ash, particles

### 4.2 Preferred lighting moods

Examples:
- blood moon glow
- cold moonlight
- ember-lit darkness
- gold mystical radiance
- storm-lit silhouette
- diffused fog light

### 4.3 What lighting should do

Lighting should:
- reveal silhouette
- emphasize motion
- create mood
- direct focus
- shape the character
- reinforce personality

Lighting is not just illumination; it is part of the storytelling.

---

## 5. Character Rendering Rules

### 5.1 Face

The face should usually be:
- partly hidden
- shadowed
- side-lit
- low-detail but expressive
- secondary to aura

Do not overly emphasize:
- clean facial beauty
- full frontal portrait realism
- overly detailed skin rendering

### 5.2 Pose

Pose should communicate character type.

Examples:
- ruler / strategist: still, controlled, composed
- warrior / fighter: active, aggressive, explosive
- assassin / agile hero: dynamic, swift, curved motion
- mystic / enchanter: floating, elegant, ritualistic

### 5.3 Costume

Costume should feel:
- layered
- flowing
- elegant
- dark-toned
- ancient Chinese inspired
- stylized rather than historically rigid

Can include:
- robes
- armor
- shoulder pieces
- layered sleeves
- belts
- tassels
- crowns / headpieces
- long cloth strips
- fur accents
- symbolic accessories

### 5.4 Cloth and silhouette

Flowing cloth is a major part of the style.

Use:
- large sleeves
- long capes
- robe hems
- ribbons
- torn cloth edges
- cloth that blends into smoke or ink

The silhouette should remain strong and iconic.

---

## 6. Background Design Rules

### 6.1 Background purpose

The background exists to support the hero, not overpower them.

Background should be:
- atmospheric
- symbolic
- simplified
- mood-driven
- slightly abstracted

### 6.2 Recommended background elements

Possible elements:
- moon / blood moon / full moon
- dark clouds
- palace silhouettes
- towers / pavilions / battlements
- battlefield haze
- banners
- spear formations
- armies in silhouette
- bamboo forest
- firelight
- river / mist
- flower petals
- falling leaves
- ink splash textures
- mountain shadows

### 6.3 Avoid

Avoid:
- overly detailed busy environments
- too many close secondary figures
- narrative clutter
- strong background elements competing with the character

---

## 7. Motion and Energy Language

Motion in WTK art is often expressed through environmental and symbolic forms rather than only body movement.

### 7.1 Motion carriers

Use:
- flowing cloth
- smoke
- ink strokes
- energy trails
- sparks
- petals
- leaves
- dust
- debris
- fog streams

### 7.2 Energy style

Energy should feel like:
- Chinese brushstroke motion
- liquid moonlight
- ink flow
- magical flame ribbons
- elegant aura currents
- restrained but powerful symbolic force

### 7.3 Direction of motion

Use motion direction to reinforce personality:
- **outward explosion** → aggression, battle fury, power release
- **inward convergence** → control, authority, strategy, political power
- **circular orbit** → elegance, wisdom, composure, mysticism
- **upward drifting** → spirituality, transcendence, ghostliness
- **sharp linear slash** → decisiveness, lethal force, speed

## 8. Color System

WTK uses a dark base with limited, personality-driven accent colors.

### 8.1 Base colors

Primary foundation:
- black
- charcoal
- smoke gray
- deep neutral darks

### 8.2 Accent color meanings

#### Crimson / Deep Red
Represents:
- war
- ambition
- pressure
- authority
- violence
- conquest
- domination

Suitable for:
- Cao Cao
- Lu Bu
- aggressive rulers / warlords / berserkers

#### Cold Blue / Moon Blue / Icy Blue
Represents:
- calm
- speed
- precision
- elegance
- moonlit combat
- distance
- discipline

Suitable for:
- Zhao Yun
- assassins
- agile heroes
- refined swordsmen

#### Muted Gold / Antique Gold
Represents:
- royalty
- destiny
- authority
- civilization
- brilliance
- poetry
- prestige

Suitable for:
- rulers
- strategists
- noble heroes
- poetic figures

#### Purple / Dark Violet
Represents:
- intrigue
- sorcery
- manipulation
- secrecy
- corruption
- psychic power

Suitable for:
- Sima Yi
- occult / control / illusion characters

#### Emerald / Jade Green
Represents:
- life
- healing
- virtue
- nature
- benevolence
- restoration

Suitable for:
- Liu Bei
- healers
- gentle or virtuous figures

#### Silver / White Mist
Represents:
- purity
- coldness
- transcendence
- frost
- sacred elegance
- isolation

Suitable for:
- celestial or refined characters
- icy or moon-related characters

### 8.3 Suggested color balance

Typical ratio:
- **80–85% dark base**
- **10–15% primary accent**
- **small amount of secondary highlight**

## 9. Hero Type Design Guidelines

### 9.1 Ruler / Warlord type

Examples:
- Cao Cao
- Sun Quan
- Liu Bei

Traits:
- stable posture
- commanding aura
- controlled movement
- symbolic authority
- palace / army / banner support
- power through presence, not frantic action

### 9.2 Warrior / General type

Examples:
- Guan Yu
- Zhang Fei
- Zhao Yun
- Ma Chao
- Lu Bu

Traits:
- stronger motion
- weapon as key focal line
- more explosive energy
- forceful silhouette
- battle-ready posture

### 9.3 Strategist / Advisor type

Examples:
- Zhuge Liang
- Zhou Yu
- Guo Jia
- Xun Yu

Traits:
- elegant, restrained movement
- fan / scroll / qin / wine cup / ritual gesture
- swirling energy instead of raw violence
- intelligence shown through calmness and control

### 9.4 Mystic / Enchanter / Occult type

Examples:
- Zhang Jiao
- Zuo Ci
- Yu Ji
- Diaochan
- Zhen Ji

Traits:
- ethereal silhouette
- dreamlike light
- mist / petals / illusion
- mysterious background
- elegance and danger combined

### 10.1 Standard hero composition
- vertical format
- single hero
- low-angle or center-dominant framing
- foreground depth elements
- middle-ground character
- background atmosphere

### 10.2 Foreground ideas

To create depth:
- blurred banners
- spear tips
- railing
- petals
- smoke
- branches
- debris
- shadow silhouettes

### 10.3 Midground

Main character:
- should dominate
- should be cleanly readable
- silhouette must remain strong

### 10.4 Background

Should remain:
- supportive
- softened
- atmospheric
- symbolic

## 11. Content Rules

### 11.1 Always prefer
- single hero focus
- strong silhouette
- unified color mood
- controlled atmosphere
- symbolic storytelling
- high-end game art feel

### 11.2 Avoid
- cartoon style
- casual fantasy design

- excessive realism
- cluttered scenes
- too many visible faces

## 12. Universal Prompt Skeleton

Use the following prompt structure when creating new hero CGs.

```text
Design a Three Kingdoms hero CG for 【Character Name】 in the unified WTK visual style.

Overall style:
Shadow Chinese Fantasy,
Ink Energy Flow,
Extreme Chiaroscuro,
Epic Three Kingdoms Hero Art.

Requirements:
- single main character
- vertical hero composition
- low-angle heroic or cinematic framing
- character occupies the main visual area
- keep the character in shadow / half-silhouette / dark contour treatment
- do not prioritize a fully clear realistic face
- focus on aura, posture, clothing, weapon, and symbolic identity
- use dramatic high-contrast lighting
- use flowing cloth, smoke, ink-like motion, and energy trails
- background should be atmospheric and symbolic, not narratively crowded
- no text, no logo, no UI

Character identity:
【role / identity】

Core temperament:
【temperament keywords】

Visual symbol:
【weapon / object / symbol】

Pose / action:
【standing / slashing / raising hand / holding fan / holding wine cup / etc.】

Environment:
【palace / battlefield / moonlit terrace / bamboo forest / fire-lit ruins / etc.】

Color system:
Use black / charcoal / smoke gray as the base.
Primary accent color: 【main accent】
Secondary accent color: 【secondary accent】

Visual atmosphere:
mysterious, powerful, cinematic, elegant, atmospheric, dark fantasy, Chinese ink style, epic hero illustration

Negative requirements:
no modern elements, no cartoon look, no extra text, no logo, no UI, no cluttered multi-character scene.
```

Who is this character?

- qin
- flame

- petals
- thunder
- banners

## 15. Example Style Mappings

### Cao Cao
- identity: chaotic-era overlord / warlord ruler
- temperament: cold, ambitious, commanding, arrogant, strategic
- symbol: sword, wine cup, banners, blood moon
- colors: black + deep crimson + muted gold

### Liu Bei
- identity: benevolent ruler
- temperament: warm, resilient, noble, compassionate, kingly
- symbol: jade, sword, lantern glow, mountain wind
- colors: black + warm gold + soft jade green

### Guan Yu
- identity: divine general of loyalty
- temperament: stern, righteous, overwhelming, sacred
- symbol: guandao, green robe, storm aura
- colors: black + deep green + antique gold

### Zhao Yun
- identity: moonlit dragon warrior
- temperament: calm, elegant, swift, precise, heroic
- symbol: spear, moonlight, water-like motion
- colors: black + icy blue + silver white

### Zhuge Liang
- identity: celestial strategist
- temperament: composed, wise, refined, transcendent
- symbol: feather fan, starlight, formation energy
- colors: black + pale cyan + moon white + light gold

### Sima Yi
- identity: shadow strategist
- temperament: cold, hidden, sinister, patient, manipulative
- symbol: raven-like smoke, dark robes, occult energy
- colors: black + dark violet + cold white

## 16. Final Global Constraint

For every hero design, always preserve the following WTK style constraint:

**Keep the WTK unified hero visual style: shadow-dominant characters, partial silhouette, extreme lighting contrast, ink-like energy flow, Chinese fantasy atmosphere, epic hero presence, abstract/symbolic background, and no text.**
