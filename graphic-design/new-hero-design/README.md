# New Hero Graphic Design

This folder contains approved redesign candidates for the brighter WTK visual direction.

These assets are **not production assets yet**.

Do not replace or rewire the current files under `public/hero-*` until the complete hero/card redesign set is reviewed and approved together.

## Direction

- brighter ancient-Chinese visual style
- clearer character silhouettes and costumes
- masked / obscured faces where approved
- hero-focused artwork without card UI baked into the image
- coordinated faction colour identity
- deliberate diversity in pose, facing direction, action, weapon use, composition, background and mounts

### Sima Yi

Asset:

```text
graphic-design/new-hero-design/sima-yi/sima-yi-new-design.avif
```

Approved direction:

- faction: Wei
- character: middle-aged Sima Yi / "Tomb Tiger" strategist
- palette: charcoal black / dark purple / old gold / cold moonlight
- orientation: restrained three-quarter profile with a watchful, calculating posture
- face: ornate dark-gold upper-face mask; mature jaw and short goatee remain visible
- weapon: no fan and no openly displayed weapon
- prop: ornate command tally / seal held low in one hand
- action: one hand behind his back, composed and patient rather than attacking
- background: moonlit Wei palace terrace, mist, soldiers and banners, with a distant wolf silhouette
- lighting: cold moonlight with restrained warm palace-fire highlights
- mount: none
- mood: calculating, patient and threatening; inspired by the "鹰视狼顾 / 隐忍待机" character concept
- composition deliberately contrasts with the brighter daylight Cao Cao, Liu Bei and Sun Quan designs
- hero-only artwork; no card UI or text baked into the image
- staged redesign candidate only; not wired into production
