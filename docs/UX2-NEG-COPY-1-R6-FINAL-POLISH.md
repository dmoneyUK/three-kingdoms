## 1. Final public/private presentation contract — latest user decisions

- **H4 = the last committed, publicly proven event or result.** Retain the real played-Duel H4 across private Negation intent/Pass and preserve genuine damage H4 across post-damage skill decisions. On successful card-gain skill, show generic verified outcome **without the actual gained card name**: e.g. `Test Player 4 used Treachery and obtained the card that caused the damage.`. This original, already-shipped sentence is **approved as-is**, irrespective of whether the gained card was earlier visible. A different skill taking from another player's Hand may say `obtained a card from [player]`, without disclosing concealed card identity. **Do NOT implement the earlier superseded UI-16 proposal** `obtained Duel` or any `publiclyProvenObtainedCardLabel`/face-name enhancement. No new schema or public card-name extraction is needed. A generic semantic effect description/count remains allowed. This avoids skill-specific public copy and Hand leaks.
- **PAC = the same public next action/wait for every authenticated viewer at the same server checkpoint.** Original neutral Negation PAC `Waiting for counter responses` + next possible cancellation/restoration explanation remains unchanged by **one viewer privately** choosing Pass or locking Negation intent. PAC must NOT say `You chose to play Negation...`, `You passed...`, nor duplicate an actual completed skill outcome. For the shown skill resolution, `Waiting for Test Player 4 to use Treachery or Skip` is correct while pending; `vgj's turn` after settlement is correct.
- **Console Decision = only this viewer's verified legal action and private state.** Move *server-confirmed own-current-window* `You chose to play Negation. Waiting for the other players.` or `You passed. Waiting for the other players.` here. No leak to other viewers' PAC, H4, Inspector, logs or spectators. These are locked *intents*, never falsely phrased as a committed public card play. All legal buttons, disabled states, Skip and timers remain server-authoritative.
- **Concise Console copy for a skill choice.** Remove redundant `YOUR DECISION` header, `Your action ·` prefix, and unnecessary `if it is still available` caveat; shown screenshot desired sentence: `Use Treachery to obtain the card that caused the damage, or Skip.`. Apply the same *generic presentation* cleanup to other providers without removing material provider requirements, target/card selection counts, or legality caveats that matter. `NO ACTION NEEDED` plus `No action needed.` should become **one** `No action needed.` (no duplicate header). Keep distinct `WAITING FOR OTHERS` only if it adds content instead of repeating the sentence.
