## 1. Authority, gameplay and privacy

1. **Server authority:** Real rules, attack legality, Duel continuation, Negation eligibility, damage, deadlines and card conservation remain unchanged. `CurrentAction` alone controls private selectable Hand cards, legal providers, Confirm, Cancel, Skip and End. Never reconstruct legal actions in React.
2. **Public event authority:** `PresentationV2`, `PresentationSnapshot` and validated public events/proofs determine genuine public card identity, played-as conversion, actor, target, committed order and settlement. Do not derive identity or causality from seat index, player name, HP change, latest animation, inferred Hand composition or merely observing a private selection. A private selected card **must not** become a public reveal.
3. **Privacy:** An opponent's Hand content, private response opportunities, hidden roles, anonymous target-card identities and skill options stay secret. Public Hand **count**, public Equipment, public Judgment and public skills may be displayed where permitted. A host may control only server-authorized test players, not take over a human participant.
4. **Seat ownership:** Actual opponent Seats and the Local Player Dock stay fixed. A public card parks in front of its **server-proven actor's** physical Seat/Dock on *each viewer's* screen; source and target are facts, never inferred from geometry.
5. **One public copy:** A committed card may change visible position/scale while transitioning but must not be simultaneously duplicated by a legacy animation, old Stage and new presenter. Any public information missing validation **fails closed**, with a readable neutral waiting state when needed, never a fictitious CardFace or an incorrect source/target.
6. **No gameplay blocking:** Presentation may not delay or stretch authoritative response windows, turn progression, damage, card submission or network updates. Timers continue from the server deadline.
7. **One-screen interaction:** The player must still see their Hand, Skills, response Guidance, timer and legal buttons. Public card animation is *informational only*; card reveal/parking must never steal interaction focus or intercept touches.

## 2. Battlefield layout — iPhone-first

The screen has three stable areas:

| Area | Responsibility | Placement |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Physical opponent Seats | Who is at the table; HP, public information, available test-player Switch | Fixed top-row or side-column topology |
| **Central battlefield** | **At most one active, newly committed public CardFace** and one short true public event message | Centred within measured safe usable battlefield, not over Seats or Guidance |
| Local Player Dock | The viewer's Hero, Skills, Equipment, private Hand and `CurrentAction` commands | Fixed at bottom; never displaced by a reveal |

### 2.1 Centre card

- During **REVEAL**, use a **readable, measured local Hand CardFace size** that is clearly larger than a parked thumbnail. Earlier code used 60×90 for Attack and 90×135 for Duel; those are historical code measurements, NOT two mandated sizes. Verify actual dimensions on phones under §8.7. Keep true art, suit, rank and identity, without a full-Stage poster.
- Place it at the measured visual centre of the usable battlefield, not halfway down the full phone viewport. Keep readable whitespace and a short message such as `Cao Cao used Attack on Zhao Yun`, using real public actor/target identifiers. The central slot must clear before the next reveal.
- No duplicated central player/target illustration; player identity remains anchored in the genuine seat.

### 2.2 Seat-front public card thumbnails

- After centre reveal, shrink **the same public card instance** to an initial medium thumbnail of approximately **48×72 CSS px** (or 50×75) with original 2:3 proportion and readable rank/suit/name.
- **Opponents in top row:** place the thumbnail **just below their actual Hero Seat**, clearly associated with that player without covering HP, name, Hand count, VIEW or SWITCH.
- **Side-column opponents:** place beside the Seat's **battlefield-facing inward edge**.
- **Local player:** place the card **immediately above the Local Dock**, on the battlefield side, not inside the private Hand or on top of Guidance/Confirm/Skip.
- Derive anchor rectangles from the actual DOM Seat/Dock identified by server player ID. Recompute safely for phone/wide/4–8 seat layouts and resize; when full size cannot fit, use contained compact layout or a small verified recent-card count. Never relocate a physical Seat to create space.
- For **ordinary Attack/Dodge and non-Duel interactions**, show the latest public card for each involved player, with at most one recent subdued predecessor as space allows; older cards may be summarized. **Exception: an active Duel retains the real Duel root and every committed Attack in full, grouped at their true author's Seat/Dock with topology-dependent orientation and a cross-player chronological sequence index (§8.1).** No latest-per-duelist replacement or `+N` substitute; never create a card without a genuine public commit.

### 2.3 Visible emphasis

- A current public card/recent newly parked card may have a restrained gold/red/blue outline based on *card or result emphasis*, not a route or link.
- Pending **Attack against the current viewer** gives the **existing Local Dock response/Guidance panel a red-accented border and readable instruction**; the entire screen should not become a blocking red modal. When the legal response ends, this border promptly retires.
- Target selection uses a normal selected Seat accent; settled outcomes belong adjacent to the **true affected player's Seat/Dock** as short labels. Colour always has accompanying readable text or an icon.
- **Hide the persistent DECK and DISCARD piles entirely from the normal battlefield (not merely shrink or move them).** Preserve real draw/discard data, card lifecycle, event animation destinations and any dedicated legal pile interaction; see §8.2.

## 3. The one public-card presentation lifecycle

For each newly **server-confirmed, visible public played-card event**:

1. **REVEAL — user-approved policy B, 2026-10-14:** A server-committed real CardFace is shown at readable Hand size in the centre for **4,000ms from its first visible large painted frame by default**. **Exception: a NEW authoritative, committed public response/counter card belonging to the active interaction may interrupt this display early**, taking centre focus immediately. It then receives its own default 4s clock, which may likewise be interrupted by an even newer committed response. Polling, phase/CurrentAction changes, speculative/failed/private choices, timeout/Pass and a mere settlement message cannot interrupt. Supersedes the older ~500–800ms timing. See §8.7.
2. **MOVE:** On normal 4s expiry, move/shrink that same CardFace over **380ms** to its true author's Seat/Dock. **On valid committed-response interruption**, start parking the older card immediately (380ms motion when practical, without delaying the NEW large card), while the newly accepted card takes centre focus right away. Avoid two simultaneous full-size centred cards, ghost duplicates and incorrect card ownership. Use no connecting stroke.
3. **PARK/WAIT:** For ordinary public reactions, keep the public thumbnail at the genuine author's Seat/Dock while waiting. **Duel exception:** preserve the full committed-card history *split by the actual author*, stacked vertically below top Seats, extended inward horizontally at left/right Seats, and horizontally above the local player's Dock (§8.1). Every card carries its real cross-player order index. Never show an uncommitted response. The Local Dock alone presents `CurrentAction`.
4. **RESPONSE:** A later genuinely committed public response gets its **own** centre reveal and then parks before **its own author**, independent of who is currently the viewer.
5. **SETTLEMENT:** Show a **server-proven** blocked/damage/negated/otherwise resolved result close to the affected real Seat/Dock. Initial result readability target **~2 seconds**; next independent proven action supersedes old display promptly. Keep the author cards long enough to understand the result; no animation can hold up gameplay.
6. **RETIRE:** Remove expired public cards, overlays and labels without a ghost copy or sudden reappearance. **An actual confirmed new response may supersede the current centre card under policy B;** merely receiving a room update or newly pending response must NOT. Maintain real history and server gameplay deadlines independently.

**Identity/dedupe requirements:** Use authoritative event/root/interaction/actor/card identities. Polling and harmless rerenders never restart OR prematurely finish a reveal. A newly observed genuine event may reveal once; historic events on reload/view-switch reconstruct parked truth. **Policy B: never queue a newly committed response behind an earlier card's remaining four seconds.** The new verified response takes centre priority, and earlier cards park at their real owners. If a poll contains multiple committed events, respect verified causal order, keep all physical identities, and let the latest eligible response take centre; never create fake cards or block game decisions.

**Reduced motion:** Do not translate/scale animate. Show a readable, full-size static centre card for **up to four seconds**, shortened only by a subsequent genuine committed response under Policy B, then switch to the true Seat/Dock without travel. Preserve accessible announcements without duplicates.

**Zero graph elements:** The new public card presenter must never render SVG relationship connectors, coloured authored/effect strokes, arrows, line branches or graph-only card nodes. Implement geometry for *positioning moving card sprites*, not for representing relationships.

## 4. Authoritative reference flows

### 4.1 Ordinary Attack → Dodge from both viewpoints

**State A: Local attacker commits Attack.** Before Confirm, only the attacker's private selected Hand card/target selection is visible. After authoritative submission, show the real Attack **centre at Hand size** to viewers for whom it is public; message `Cao Cao used Attack on Zhao Yun`. After its normal 4s or a genuine new committed response interrupt (§8.7), shrink/park it **above Cao Cao's Dock** in Cao Cao's view, or **below Cao Cao's real opponent Seat** in other players' views. The legitimate target and responder are not deduced by watching the animation.

**State B: Defender sees incoming Attack.** In Zhao Yun's viewpoint, the newly visible genuine Attack first appears **in the centre**, accompanied by `Cao Cao used Attack on you`. It parks when the default hold ends or a real committed response interrupts, at **Cao Cao's true opponent Seat**, not at Zhao Yun. Zhao Yun's existing Local Dock response/Guidance acquires a red accented border and a clear short message, e.g. `Cao Cao attacked you. Play Dodge or Skip.` Show the real response timer and only the options offered by `CurrentAction`. A normal Attack is **not** generally playable as Dodge; physical Attack `AS DODGE` (Zhao Yun Braveheart/Longdan) appears only where the server genuinely allows conversion. Do not reveal a privately selected Dodge or invent a Dodge placeholder.

**State C: Defender confirms Dodge.** After server confirmation, the newly committed Dodge immediately supersedes any still-revealing Attack (§8.7). The publicly played genuine Dodge (or an actually proven physical Attack-as-Dodge with correct displayed physical art and played-as label) is shown **centre-first at Hand size** for each viewer. Message `Zhao Yun played Dodge`. Then shrink/park it **above Zhao Yun's Dock** in Zhao Yun's own view, or **beneath Zhao Yun's Hero Seat** in Cao Cao's and observers' views. Keep the older Attack thumbnail at Cao Cao's seat as subdued context until the blocked result is readable. No counter-effect line.

**State D1: Attack blocked.** When verified, show `DODGED` / `Attack blocked` at the true defender seat; leave recent Attack/Dodge thumbnails readable briefly, then retire. Never show `TAKE DAMAGE` simultaneously with a proved successful Dodge.

**State D2: Defender Skip/timeout/no legal Dodge.** Skip itself is **not** proof that damage happened. When server proof establishes real Attack damage, show `TAKE DAMAGE` adjacent to Zhao Yun's actual Seat/Dock (and accurate real HP state). Maintain a readable ~2s result, visible to attacker/defender/observer, even if the response round advanced quickly. If prevention/redirect or a special skill changes damage, announce only the actually proven outcome. Do not display a fictitious physical response card.

**All perspectives:** The same true card/actor/result identities must match; placement is different because physical Seats rotate for the viewer. The Local Dock highlight is **only** for the viewer with an actual pending decision. A spectator does not see or activate the defender's legal private Hand.

### 4.2 Duel

- Genuine publicly committed Duel stratagem: **Policy-B centre reveal (normally four seconds, interruptible by a new committed response)** then **first persistent card under/along the real Duel author's Seat/Dock**. During the shared Negation opportunity the genuine root Duel stays visible; uncommitted Negation intents are private. Do not display gigantic Duel/EFFECT/NEGATION panels.
- Once DUEL_EXCHANGE is authoritative, each genuinely committed Attack gets its own **Policy-B centre reveal**, which may interrupt the previous card, then **appends after that same author's previous cards** in their own anchored history. All players see actual server-proven card author and card identity, never a guessed alternation; Dodge cannot answer Duel.
- **Direction by physical seat topology (§8.1):** top-row duelist → cards vertically beneath their Hero; left-side duelist → horizontally inward to the right; right-side duelist → horizontally inward to the left; local duelist → horizontally across the battlefield immediately above the Local Dock. The spectator's screen can therefore contain **two distinct seat-attached Duel histories** simultaneously. No single global bottom Dock rail.
- **Chronology across both participants:** The actual root Duel is event #1; each subsequently confirmed Attack is #2, #3, #4, etc. Add compact globally unique order badges to the physical CardFaces so that a spectator knows whether Cao Cao or Zhen Ji answered next even when their cards are in separate columns. Never replace a previous card by its author or collapse history into latest-only +N.
- Histories are locally scrollable in their **own orientation** when many cards exceed safe battlefield space, never over hero portraits, other seats, response timer, central reveal, Hand, Guidance or Confirm/Skip. Keep proven settlement visible ~2s before orderly retirement. No second public CardFace, fabricated pending Attack, connector/graph or reopened root reveal on refresh.
### 4.3 Negation and counter-Negation

- With an open Negation opportunity but **no committed Negation**, show **only the original public action's card** in its genuine current centre-reveal or actor-parked phase and one compact public effect sentence; no provisional Negation CardFace, no private responder leak, no verbose `NEGATION RESPONSE`, `EFFECT` or `REACTION CHAIN` panel.
- Each newly confirmed Negation/counter-Negation may interrupt the previous centre card (§8.7); it shows the real CardFace **at centre then at its author's seat**, with the most recent public response strongest. Older same-interaction cards remain subdued/compact and a history count may replace overflow.
- Only server-proof outcome decides `effect negated`, `effect restored` or continuation. Preserve per-viewer private Local Dock's `Play Negation or Pass` (or `Pass` only) options and one shared, server-authoritative window deadline. Never infer parity of the public counter chain to change game rules.

### 4.4 Group/AOE and other public cards

- Root Barbarian Invasion, Raining Arrows, Oath etc. genuine public played card: **Policy-B centre reveal** → source's owner-side thumbnail. Subsequent *actually committed* Attack/Dodge/Negation from eligible participants reveal individually and park before each true author. Compact per-player `waiting / responded / avoided / damaged` information must be backed by real public statuses; no imaginary responses or per-target connectors.
- For a current Raining Arrows defender, use the authoritative Dodge providers; keep unrelated Hand cards visible but subdued. If no legal Dodge is available, the explicit local choice may be `TAKE DAMAGE` (per server-projected legal action), **not** a meaningless disabled Confirm. Numeric damage is never guessed.
- Genuine event-specific card displays (equipment, Judgment, Private Draw, Bumper Harvest, theft/discard selection, Hero skill continuations) keep their own rules in §5. No fabricated CardFace for skills without a real publicly played physical card. Generic skill notices remain small and nonblocking.

### 9.1 Deterministic window protocol

1. **Open:** When an effect may be Negated, create one server-authoritative confidential window for **ALL living seats**, irrespective of private cards/skill providers. Persist opaque windowId, canonical seat-priority start, ordered snapshot of the living seats, effect/root causal identity, openedAt, **immutable maximum deadline = openedAt + 8,000ms** and private submissions. Keep existing source/target starting anchors for ordinary Stratagem, Judgement, Group/AOE and Harvest. Everyone sees the same remaining clock until settlement; nobody sees others' cards, intent type, submitted seats or completion count.
2. **Offer in parallel to everyone:** All living players receive their own private CurrentAction. If the server finds any genuinely legal Negation card or Hero-skill/conversion provider, offer those legitimate options PLUS Pass; otherwise offer **Pass ONLY**. **Never automatically submit Pass just because the player lacks a provider.** The no-card player must explicitly tap Pass for the window to close early; doing nothing is allowed and becomes implicit Pass only at 8s timeout. Confirm stores a secret intent, not a public played card; unrelated actions remain disabled.
3. **Individual lock and early-finish eligibility:** A server-accepted Negation intent **or explicit Pass** is persisted and locked for that player's window; show only to them “Response submitted — waiting”. Duplicate identical submission stays idempotent, conflict rejects. **After each successfully persisted submission**, the server may check whether **every ID in the original living-seat snapshot** has a valid persisted response. If yes, claim/settle this window immediately without waiting for T0+8s. If any seat is missing, keep the original deadline; neither polling nor a client's guess may close it. **Never auto-submit Pass or remove no-card players from this check.** Do not publish completion counts, submitted IDs or individual choices.
4. **Close at the earlier of two authoritative conditions:** **(A)** every living seat in the window snapshot has **explicitly persisted Negation intent OR Pass**, or **(B)** the immutable T0+8s deadline is reached. The server, not React/polling, atomically claims settlement once and validates all accepted submissions; if condition A was reached at time T, do not accept late duplicate/conflicting writes after the claim. The actual winner is the first valid Negation by canonical seat order, **NEVER the fastest submitter**. Losing intents do not cost cards. If no valid winner, settle the underlying effect. Concurrent final submits, timer expiry and retries must not double-close, consume twice or skip a legitimate responder.
5. **Commit one physical card:** For the winning intent only, perform the usual authoritative card consumption (exactly once), Negation effect toggle, log/public card event, real actor identity and causal history. All losing intents are voided with **zero cards consumed**, even if they submitted earlier/later, and no fake card is revealed. Each player's own private final status can reflect whether their intent was used; other players see only the accepted public card. Ensure card conservation and existing hero provider legality.
6. **Counter window:** If a Negation wins, open a NEW shared 8-second window against that actual Negation. Seat-order priority starts with the **next living seat after the actual committed Negation's actor**, wrapping around; include every living seat (including the last actor upon wrap-around, if a legal provider still exists). All previous losing intents are discarded, **never automatically carried over** as counter-Negations, because the tactical decision/target is different. Repeat for counter-counter-Negation without arbitrary chain-depth cutoff; preserve genuine legal game termination and event proof.
7. **Final settlement:** When a window closes with no accepted Negation, settle the current negated/restored effect exactly once. A Duel must NEVER begin its Attack exchange until the root shared window **and every genuine counter window** complete. Preserve per-target cancellation semantics for multi-target effects.

### 9.2 Concrete seat example and priority

Five seats clockwise **1→2→3→4→5**; seat 1 plays Duel against seat 3.

- Shared window #1: all five living seats privately receive Negation/Pass; seats 2 and 4 submit actual Negation intents, seats 1, 3 and 5 **explicitly Pass**. When the **last** of these five submissions is persisted (possibly before 8s), the server settles early: **seat 2 wins** by seat priority, only seat 2's Negation becomes public, seat 4 retains their card. If seat 5 stays silent, the window instead remains open until 8s; the winner is still seat 2.

- After the first or any subsequent shared window that closes with no successful new Negation, resolve the current root effect state. Seat 3 starts the Duel Attack exchange **only if Duel ultimately remains active**.
- If only seat 4 submits Negation and all other seats **explicitly Pass**, seat 4 wins **immediately after the last response**. If others are silent, the server waits until 8s even if they lack Negation cards. If everyone Passes explicitly, settle early with no winner; if nobody responds, settle at 8s. No seat-by-seat 8s turns.
